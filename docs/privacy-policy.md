# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 July 2026**

Pack Lite does not collect any of your data. There is no account to create, no
analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Your packing lists stay
on your device.

This policy explains that in more detail, including the few places your data can
travel because *you* or *your phone* moved it there.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The developer of Pack Lite receives no information about you or your
use of the app. There is no server to send it to.

Specifically, Pack Lite does not collect or transmit:

- your name, email address, or any account details — there are no accounts
- your packing lists, items, or settings
- analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or advertising identifiers
- your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data — the app
  never requests access to any of these

## Where your data is stored

Everything you create in Pack Lite — your lists, categories, items, presets, and
your theme and vibration settings — is stored **on your device**, in storage that
belongs to the app. It is not uploaded to us.

## Ways your data can leave your device

There are three, and you control all of them.

### 1. Your phone's own backup

**Android.** Pack Lite allows Android's standard Auto Backup, so your app data
may be included in the device backup your phone makes to **your own Google
account**. This is a feature of Android, not something Pack Lite uploads: it is
what lets your lists reappear when you set up a new phone. The developer has no
access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings under Backup, and
Google's handling of it is covered by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**iOS.** In the same way, your app data may be included in your device's iCloud
or computer backup, under your own Apple account, subject to
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Using **Delete All Data** in Settings clears your data on the device and asks the
system to update its backup copy. Note that this is a request the operating
system schedules — it is not instantaneous, and it cannot happen while your
device is offline.

### 2. Export

**Export Data** writes your lists to a JSON file and hands it to your device's
share sheet. Where it goes next is entirely your choice — saved to Files, sent to
yourself, or anywhere else. Once you have shared it, that copy is governed by
whatever service you sent it to. Pack Lite does not keep or transmit a copy.

**Import Data** reads a file you pick. Nothing is read without you choosing it.

### 3. Links out of the app

Settings contains links to the project's page on GitHub and to GitHub Sponsors.
Opening either leaves Pack Lite and takes you to GitHub, where
[GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
applies.

If you choose to sponsor the project, **GitHub handles the entire transaction**.
Pack Lite never sees your payment details, and the app contains no purchases of
any kind.

## Children

Pack Lite collects no personal information from anyone, including children under
13. There is no account, no profile, and no communication feature.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to show you, correct,
export, or delete — requests of that kind have no data to act on. Your data is
yours and it is on your device:

- **Export it** at any time from Settings → Export Data
- **Delete it** with Settings → Delete All Data, or by uninstalling the app
- **Stop the backup copy** in your device's system settings
